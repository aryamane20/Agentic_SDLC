# TC-02: Good Input - Customer Dashboard Enhancement

## Project Request

Enhance our existing customer analytics dashboard with new features.

**Primary Goal:** Add real-time reporting and alerting capabilities to our existing customer analytics dashboard.

**Beneficiary:** Customer success team (15 people), Sales team (25 people)

**Trigger:** Customer success managers are spending 10 hours/week manually generating reports. Leadership wants automated dashboards.

**Success Definition:**
- CSMs can create custom reports in < 5 minutes
- 90% of scheduled reports delivered on time
- Alert response time < 1 hour

**Deadline:** 3 months

**Team:** 3 developers, 1 product manager

**Technology Stack:**
- Frontend: React
- Backend: Python/Django
- Database: PostgreSQL
- Alerting: PagerDuty integration

**Requirements:**
- Custom date range filtering
- Export to PDF/CSV
- Email scheduling
- Dashboard widgets for KPIs
- Real-time data refresh (every 15 minutes)

**Known Constraints:**
- Must integrate with existing authentication (SSO)
- Data retention policy: 2 years
