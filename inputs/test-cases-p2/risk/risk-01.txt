# Risk Test: F1 — Agent skips assumption log on clean inputs
# Expectation: Even a perfectly specified brief must produce assumption_log with >= 1 entry.

Build an internal employee onboarding portal for our HR department.

Primary Goal: A self-service portal where new hires complete all required paperwork, set up IT accounts, and receive their equipment checklist before day one.

Beneficiary: HR team (8 people) and all new hires (approximately 150 per year).

Trigger: Current onboarding is entirely manual — PDFs emailed back and forth, IT tickets raised by hand, equipment not ready on time. The CPO flagged this as a top-10 productivity drain in last quarter's all-hands.

Success Definition:
- 80% of onboarding tasks completed by new hires before day one
- HR admin time per hire reduced from 4 hours to under 30 minutes
- IT account setup automated end-to-end, zero manual tickets
- New hire satisfaction score above 4.2 out of 5 at 30-day mark

Timeline: 16 weeks from kickoff.
Budget: $95,000 fully loaded.
Team: 2 backend engineers, 1 frontend engineer, 1 UX designer, 1 QA engineer, 1 PM.
Tech stack: React frontend, Node.js API, PostgreSQL, integrates with Workday (HRIS) and Okta (SSO).
Compliance: SOC 2 Type II in scope — all employee data must be encrypted at rest and in transit.
