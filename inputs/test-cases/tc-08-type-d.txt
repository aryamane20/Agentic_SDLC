# TC-08: Type D - System Integration

## Project Request

**Primary Goal:** Integrate Salesforce with NetSuite for unified customer view.

**Beneficiary:** Sales team, Finance team

**Trigger:** Sales and Finance use separate systems. Revenue recognition is manual. Customer service can't see sales history.

**Success Definition:**
- Customer data syncs bidirectionally between systems
- Sales orders automatically create invoices in NetSuite
- Customer support has full view of customer history
- Reconciliation automated

**Deadline:** 4 months

**Budget:** $60,000

**Team:** 2 integration specialists, 1 Salesforce admin, 1 NetSuite admin

**Systems:**
- Salesforce (CRM)
- NetSuite (ERP)
- Custom customer portal (existing)

**Requirements:**
- Bidirectional sync of customer records
- Order creation in Salesforce → Invoice in NetSuite
- Invoice payment status sync back to Salesforce
- Contact and opportunity sync
- Error handling and retry logic
- Audit log of all sync activities
- Field mapping configuration UI

**Integration Points:**
- Customer records (bidirectional)
- Contact records (bidirectional)
- Opportunities → Sales Orders (Salesforce → NetSuite)
- Invoices → Payments (NetSuite → Salesforce)
- Products catalog (NetSuite → Salesforce)

**Constraints:**
- Zero data loss tolerance
- Sync latency < 15 minutes
- Must handle 10,000+ records
- Both systems have custom fields that must map
