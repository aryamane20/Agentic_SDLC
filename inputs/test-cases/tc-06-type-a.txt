# TC-06: Type A - New Internal Tool

## Project Request

**Primary Goal:** Build an internal employee directory with org chart visualization.

**Beneficiary:** All 500 employees

**Trigger:** HR can't answer "who reports to who" questions easily. People can't find experts in specific areas.

**Success Definition:**
- All employees searchable by name, role, team, skills
- Org chart accurate within 1 business day of changes
- Employee profiles 95% complete

**Deadline:** 3 months

**Budget:** $40,000

**Team:** 2 developers, 1 designer

**Requirements:**
- Search by name, title, department, skills
- Org chart visualization (interactive)
- Employee profiles (photo, title, team, manager, skills, contact)
- Integration with Workday for employee data
- Mobile responsive
- Quick links to Slack, calendar

**Constraints:**
- Must be SOC 2 compliant
- Data must sync daily from Workday
